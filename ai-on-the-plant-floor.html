Free Guide — AI on the Plant Floor

What AI Actually Does on the Plant Floor

A machine already records vibration, current, temperature and pressure — plus the test result for every part it makes. This guide explains, in plain words, what AI does with all that recorded data, and what it does not do.

Free PDF download — 6 pages. Enter your name and email below and we will send the guide straight to your inbox.

The watchdog that learns “normal”

AI learns what a good cycle looks like — all the sensors together — and flags slow drift long before a fixed alarm limit is crossed.

Predicting the part, not just the machine

Because machine data and part test data are recorded together, AI can flag a cycle heading for a bad part while the part is still being made.

Root cause, without the hunting

AI compares good cycles with bad ones and narrows the cause to one or two possibilities, so a person can confirm it in minutes.

Tuning and optimisation

AI notices when the process drifts out of spec and suggests where to look. The PLC still makes the changes, and a person still approves them.

The operator’s assistant

A local assistant that answers plain questions from the machine’s own recorded history — not a chatbot on the internet.

Rules-based vs. data-based, edge vs. cloud

Why the PLC stays rules-based, why AI is data-based, and why the AI in this scenario runs locally on the plant network — offline, with the data staying in the plant.
